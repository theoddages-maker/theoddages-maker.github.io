City guides for your first visit

The Odd Ages makes complete city guides for first-time visitors: a 3-day route with options for 5 and 7 days, the food worth eating, and dated prices on screen. Full guides are on YouTube; short clips go out on YouTube Shorts, Instagram and TikTok as @the_odd_ages.

Our tools

We run a few small tools of our own to publish videos and to keep an eye on how the channel is doing. They work only with The Odd Ages' own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. They are not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to them. What they read and keep is in the privacy policy.
