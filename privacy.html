Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy covers the tools The Odd Ages uses to publish videos and to track its own channels. The tools serve only The Odd Ages' own accounts; no outside user signs in to them.

Who is responsible

The Odd Ages, run by Ruben. Contact: theoddages@gmail.com.

What the tools access

  • YouTube (through Google sign-in): the channel's name, subscriber and view counts, YouTube Analytics reports, and comments on the channel's videos, so we know which ones still need a reply. The upload tool also uploads the channel's own videos, as private, and sets their thumbnails.
  • Instagram (through Facebook Login): the @the_odd_ages account's name, follower count, its posts and their insights, and comments on those posts. The publishing tool uploads and publishes the account's own Reels.
  • TikTok (through Login Kit): the @the_odd_ages account's profile, follower and like counts, and its list of videos with their view counts.

How we use it

Only to publish our own videos and to show our own figures on a private dashboard. The dashboard shows counts only: how many comments wait for a reply, never their text or who wrote them. We do not sell, share or rent any of this data, use it for advertising, or combine it with other sources.

Where it is kept

Sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer, never on a server or in files. The figures are kept on that computer and in the private dashboard. We keep past figures to show trends and delete them when they are no longer useful.

Comments from viewers

If you comment on one of our videos, the tools read the comment's text, author name and time to work out whether it still needs a reply. This is only kept for that purpose and never published. To have anything removed, write to theoddages@gmail.com.

Google user data

The Odd Ages' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tools use the YouTube API Services; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Withdrawing access

Access can be removed at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/connections, for Facebook and Instagram under Settings, Business integrations, and for TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and complain to a data protection authority. Write to theoddages@gmail.com.

Changes

We update this page when the tools change and note the date at the top.
